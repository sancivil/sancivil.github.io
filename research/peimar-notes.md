## Warranty per series

Products-services page:
- S Series: 30 years linear power warranty, 30 years product warranty.
- OR & DR Series: 30 years linear power warranty, 25 years product warranty.

Flyer: "All our panels are provided with a 30-year linear production guarantee and a 30-year product warranty (for the Made in Italy modules) and 25-years (for the half-cell)."

Warranty PDF 2026 (Peimar Srl), power output loss limits over 30 years:
- Polycrystalline: 2% in the first year, then 0.41%/year, ending at 86.11% in year 30.
- Mono PERC: 3% in the first year, then 0.41%/year, ending at 85.11% in year 30.
- Mono TOPCon: 1% in the first year, then 0.3%/year, ending at 90.30% in year 30.
- The warranty starts on the delivery date (Incoterms 2020) to the buyer.

Batteries: 10 years on the PSI-X-5.8MST-V2, 5.8SLV-V2, PSI-X-HS and PSI-A-BT3.8S datasheets. PSI-A: "valid only upon product registration on the portal and activation of the monitoring service."

## Technology wording Peimar uses

Peimar gives no prose explanations of these technologies on its site. Only these datasheet and flyer labels exist:
- S Series (Made in Italy): "Made in Italy module", "Manufactured in our production factory", "PERC monofacial technology" (SA500M), "PERC bifacial technology" (SA550M (B)), "TOPCon monofacial technology" with "MONO M10 | N-TYPE" (SA485MN), "Anti-reflective glass", "Maximum efficiency and high performance", "Compact and sturdy frame", "Anchorable also on the short side". SF360M (FG) adds "PID free" and "Consistent long-term performance".
- OR Series (flyer heading "Half Cell"): "Extra-EU module", "TOPCon bifacial technology", "Anti-reflective double glass", cells "MONO M10 HALF | N-TYPE" (OR10H…MNDB), "MONO M12 HALF | N-TYPE" (OR12H715MNDB), "MONO M10 | N-TYPE", 96 "M10 CUT" cells (OR10E460MNDB). OR10H375M (FR): "MONO M10 HALF | PERC", "PERC monofacial technology".
- Glass: the S Series uses 3.2 mm low-iron tempered glass. OR double-glass models use 2.0 mm low-iron tempered glass on the back.
- Fire: Class 1 (UNI 9177), B-s1, d0 (EN 13501-1), BROOF(t1) (EN 13501-5). RoHS 2011/65/UE.
- Flyer frame and backsheet options: Anodized Aluminium Frames in Black, Silver, Red (RAL 8015). Tempered Glass: Transparent, Red (RAL 8015). Backsheet: Black, White.
- Not found anywhere in the sources: "multi-busbar", "full black", and prose explanations of N-type, TOPCon, half-cut or bifacial.
- Certifications (modules page): IEC 61215 / 61730 (Kiwa for the S Series, TÜV for the OR Series), PID free (IEC TS 62804-1), ammonia (IEC 62716), salt mist (IEC 61701), dust and sand (IEC 60068-2-68, OR double glass), UL61730 (CSA), ADQCC Abu Dhabi certificate of conformity. The Italian site holds ISO 9001:2015, ISO 14001:2015 and ISO 45001:2018.

## Model suffixes

Peimar does not publish a key to its model codes. What the datasheets show for the sampled models:
- (FR): "Red Line module", Red (RAL 8015) frame, red coloured glass ("treatment of pigmented nanoparticles"). Seen on OR10H375M (FR).
- (FG): "Green Line", Green (RAL 6007), coloured glass. Seen on SF360M (FG).
- (BF): black frame with white backsheet on SA500M (BF). Black frame on the double-glass OR10H450MNDB (BF).
- (FB): black frame with black backsheet on SA500M (FB). Black frame on the double-glass OR10H450MNDB (FB). For double-glass models the sheets do not show how FB differs from BF.
- (SF): silver frame on OR10H440MNDB (SF).
- (B): silver frame with transparent backsheet, "PERC bifacial" on SA550M (B).
- No suffix: silver frame on OR10H595MNDB and OR12H715MNDB.
- Certificate labels: "Polycrystalline (SGXXXP)", "Monocrystalline (SGXXXM / SMXXXM / SFXXXM / SAXXXM)", "Half-cell (OR10HXXXM)", "Monocrystalline (OR Series MNDB)". All sampled MNDB models are "TOPCon bifacial" with double glass, and SA…MN is "TOPCon monofacial N-type". Peimar never spells out the letters themselves.
- panels.json therefore sets `finish.label` only for FR (Red Line) and FG (Green Line).

## Peimar Designer data caveats

- The Designer API holds several datasheet versions of the same model, and the values can differ between them. For OR10H560M, the untagged version has ctVoc -0.28 and ctIsc 0.042. IT_2025_06_01 has ctVoc -0.25 and ctIsc 0.046. panels.json keeps the newest version.
- The API lists SG310M (BF) at 315 W, which matches SG315M (BF).
- Spot check: the current SA500M (BF) datasheet (EN_2026_07_00) gives Vmp 37.77 V, Imp 13.24 A, Voc 46.46 V, Isc 14.03 A and 21.33% efficiency. These match the panels.json record (version IT_2025_06_02).
