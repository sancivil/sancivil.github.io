# Faraday (faradayiq.com): recovered legacy content

The live site shows a PHP error, so everything here comes from Internet Archive snapshots. Snapshots from mid-2025 onward are bot-check ("One moment, please...") pages or a `/lander` parking redirect. The good captures are the ones listed below. The old site was WordPress with Elementor and the "Theme" blog theme. Posts are credited to "Daban" (author slug `sevar-fareq`), and the only archive month is April 2022.

faradayelect.com, the older domain, has only a bot-check page archived (20230720040400). It adds no about, team, partner or certificate content. Some images on faradayiq.com still load from faradayelect.com URLs.
